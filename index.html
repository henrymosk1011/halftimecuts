01The Shop

First half, 2005.
Second half, now.

Halftime Cuts opened in 2005 as East Pasadena’s neighborhood sports-themed men’s shop. Under new ownership and renovated top to bottom, we’re playing the second half at a higher level — sharp cuts, hot towel shaves and good conversation, without the high prices.

Meet the owner

George

Before taking over Halftime, George spent years behind the chair helping grow successful barbershops in Burbank and the San Fernando Valley. He renovated the shop top to bottom and rebuilt the vibe around two things: the craft, and the community.

The goal every time is simple — you walk out sharper, and more confident, than you walked in.
