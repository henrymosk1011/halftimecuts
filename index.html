01The Shop

First half, 2005.
Second half, now.

Halftime Cuts opened in 2005 as East Pasadena’s neighborhood sports-themed men’s shop. Under new ownership and renovated top to bottom, we’re playing the second half at a higher level — sharp cuts, hot towel shaves and good conversation, without the high prices.

Meet the owner

George

Before taking over Halftime, George spent years behind the chair helping grow successful barbershops in Burbank and the San Fernando Valley. He renovated the shop top to bottom and rebuilt the vibe around two things: the craft, and the community.

The goal every time is simple — you walk out sharper, and more confident, than you walked in.

08FAQ

Good to know.

Quick answers before you pull up. Anything else? Call (626) 796-4253.

01How do I book an appointment at Halftime Cuts?

Book online any time through our Square booking page — pick your barber, your service and a time that works. Prefer to talk to someone? Call us at (626) 796-4253.

02How much is a haircut?

A haircut is $45 and takes about 30 minutes. A haircut with a beard trim is $65, and a haircut with the full beard service or a hot towel shave is $80.

03Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids cuts are available — you’ll see current pricing and open times when you book. Stef is a favorite with younger clients.

04What are your hours?

We’re open Tuesday through Saturday, 9am to 6pm, and closed on Sunday and Monday.

05Where is Halftime Cuts located?

We’re at 3205 E Foothill Blvd, Pasadena, CA 91107 — in East Pasadena near the 210, a few minutes from Sierra Madre, Arcadia and Altadena.

06Do you offer beard trims and hot towel shaves?

Yes. We do beard trims ($25), a full beard service with shape, lineup and neck shave ($40), and classic hot towel shaves ($50).
